# Party Interaction Plan (Phase C)

## Camp & Travel Conversations
- **Orin–Mira:** Map corrections, quiet questions about home, shared worry about names slipping.
- **Kael–Orin:** Short checks (“Masih ingat jalan pulang?”). Kael rarely starts; Orin does.
- **Mira–Iven:** Route arguments that become useful. She draws; he walks.
- **Kael–Tavi:** He tells her to stop touching things. She does it anyway. Later he asks what she found.
- **Tavi–Sera (when present):** Ethics of waking old machines.
- **Neris–party:** Songs at night that make someone remember a name they had forgotten.

## Reactions to Major Events
- **Ch.5 surge:** Mira steadies Orin. Kael (later) asks what it felt like and does not like the answer.
- **Ch.10 sky:** Iven jokes about wrong constellations until he realizes his next delivery address may not exist.
- **Ch.14 personal wounds:** Party does not force speeches. Silence is allowed. Mira and Orin sit closer.
- **Ch.18 report truth:** Kael expects blame. Orin refuses to give it. Tavi asks practical questions about the falsified orders.
- **Ch.20 Vaelor:** Disagreement possible. Kael is hardest to sway toward any mercy. Mira wants evidence. Orin asks what Vaelor lost.

## Conflict Resolution
- Arguments end with a concrete next step, not forced agreement.
- No character is required to like every decision.
- Support is shown by action (standing guard, fixing a map, sharing food) more than speeches.

## Character-Specific Beats
- Orin avoids goodbyes → forced to say one in late game.
- Mira overcommits → learns a map can be incomplete and still true.
- Kael guilt → accepts that remembering the failure is the point.
- Iven cynicism → chooses a side when a route vanishes for good.
- Tavi play → faces consequence when a “toy” harms someone.
