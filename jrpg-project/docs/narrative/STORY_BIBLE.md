# Story Bible — THE ATLAS OF UNREMEMBERED STARS

## Pitch

In the world of **Lumerra**, memory itself is fraying. Villages forget their founders. Maps disagree with the land. People wake missing names of those they love. At the heart of this quiet catastrophe is **The Stillness** — an ancient force once bound by a forgotten accord — and a man who believes erasing pain will save the world.

**Orin Vale**, a youth with an unusual sensitivity to **Resonance** (the living magic of places, people, and memory), leaves the village of Ashwillow after a fragment of Resonance awakens beneath the blue sky. With cartographers, guards, archivists, and wanderers, Orin traces erased history across shattered kingdoms, buried libraries, and the Heart of Lumerra — confronting **Vaelor Senn**, the Curator, whose solution may unmake everything worth saving.

## Themes

| Theme | Expression |
|-------|------------|
| Memory & identity | Who are we if our past is taken? |
| Cost of healing | Restoring memory can wound |
| Responsibility of knowledge | Archives, maps, and lies |
| Found family | Party bonds across six arcs |
| Mercy vs control | Curator’s tragedy vs tyranny |
| Wonder | Magical creatures, lost cities, star-fall nights |

Tone: hopeful adventure with genuine loss; humor in travel banter; not unrelenting grimdark.

## Structure

- **30 main chapters** in **6 arcs**
- Side content is optional; main path remains coherent if side quests are skipped
- Ending is fixed in outcome class (Stillness stopped / world can remember) with epilogue variations from relationships and major choices

## Central Question

Is a world without pain worth a world without truth?

## Non-Goals

- Multiplayer narrative
- Full open branching campaign (engine supports limited choice consequences)
- Copying external game plots or characters
