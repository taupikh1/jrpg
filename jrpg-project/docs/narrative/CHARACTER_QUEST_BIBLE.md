# Character Quest Bible (Phase F)
**Personal content for every playable character. Connected to main mysteries but not required for story completion.**

## Orin — Jejak Badai Tenang
- **ID:** `quest_char_orin_badai`
- **Available:** After Chapter 14
- **Start:** Orin brings up the quiet storm unprompted at camp
- **Objectives:**
  1. Return to Ashwillow well (`char_orin_well_revisited`)
  2. Find elder’s old account (`char_orin_account_found`)
  3. Cross-check with lattice incident clues in Arc V (`char_orin_lattice_link`)
  4. Conclude (`char_orin_resolved`)
- **Reward:** Personal accessory “Jubah Tambal yang Diingat” + story flag
- **Resolution:** Parents lost in a localized lattice failure, not weather.

## Mira — Peta yang Tidak Ada
- **ID:** `quest_char_mira_peta`
- **Available:** After Chapter 14
- **Start:** Mira shows the impossible chart
- **Objectives:**
  1. Reconstruct 3 missing coordinates
  2. Visit the “non-existent” site on Echo Road
  3. Recover sister’s final note
  4. Resolve (`char_mira_resolved`)
- **Reward:** Improved map item + flag
- **Resolution:** Sister folded into a lattice scar; not fully recoverable, but named.

## Kael — Tiga Laporan
- **ID:** `quest_char_kael_laporan`
- **Available:** After Chapter 6; climaxes Chapter 18
- **Objectives:**
  1. Recover first draft
  2. Find altered second version
  3. Confront the order that buried the third (`ch18_report_exposed` shared)
- **Reward:** Kael’s old cloak upgraded + trust flag
- **Resolution:** Report falsified for crown stability.

## Iven — Alamat Terakhir
- **ID:** `quest_char_iven_alamat`
- **Available:** After Chapter 7
- **Objectives:** Deliver one last letter to an address that keeps moving → choose to stop or finish
- **Reward:** Route charm
- **Resolution:** Some places are being un-written; he chooses to remember the route anyway.

## Tavi — Nyanyian yang Terbangun
- **ID:** `quest_char_tavi_nyanyian`
- **Available:** After Chapter 8
- **Objectives:** Stabilize the toy without letting it fully wake a larger construct
- **Reward:** Unique utility skill or accessory
- **Resolution:** She learns invention has memory of its own.

Supporting characters (Sera, Neris, Bram) receive shorter personal scenes tied to their join/appearance chapters once runtime IDs are approved. No new runtime IDs created here.
