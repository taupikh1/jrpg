# World & Location Bible — Expanded (Stage 6)
**Status:** Newly written expansion. Builds on WORLD_BIBLE.md, REGIONAL_BIBLE.md, and world-registry.js.  
**Language:** Location flavor and readable text samples in Bahasa Indonesia.

## World Identity (Lumerra)
Resonance is the living relation between people, place, and memory. When it frays, names and places begin to slip. The lattice was built to hold memory steady; its failure created the First Silence. The Accord chose to bury the tools and rewrite history rather than face the wound.

## Regional Expansion

### region_ashwillow — Lembah Ashwillow
- **Hub:** Desa Ashwillow
- **Playable zone:** `village`
- **Flavor:** Sungai yang tenang, ladang, festival yang namanya mulai dilupakan.
- **Readable sample:** Papan pengumuman desa — “Festival Pendiri... (nama terhapus). Silakan tanya tetua.”
- **Resonance:** Soft node near the old well. First symptoms appear here.

### region_whispering_wood — Hutan Berbisik
- **Playable zone:** `forest`
- **Flavor:** Carving setengah terhapus di pohon tua. Suara angin yang kadang menyebut nama.
- **Key:** Mira joins. Glyphs foreshadow lattice language.

### region_bellhaven — Wilayah Bellhaven
- **Playable zones:** `road_bellhaven`, `bellhaven_gate`, `bellhaven_plaza`, `bellhaven_market`, `bellhaven_archive_hall`, `bellhaven_archive_stacks`, `bellhaven_archive_restricted`
- **Flavor:** Kota perdagangan. Arsip yang sengaja dikosongkan.
- **Readable sample:** Rak kosong bertuliskan “Dipindahkan atas perintah Accord — catatan tidak diperlukan.”

## Connection Notes

## Design Principles (preserved)
- Places feel inhabited before the plot arrives.
- Memory loss is personal before it is apocalyptic.
- Every region ties to at least one mystery or character beat.
