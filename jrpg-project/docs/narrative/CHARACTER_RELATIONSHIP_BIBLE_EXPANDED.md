## Principal Party — Voice & Relationship Deepening

### Orin Vale (`orin` / `character_orin_vale`)
**Voice sample:**  
“Aku tidak minta dunia jadi sempurna. Aku cuma minta orang-orang tidak kehilangan nama orang yang mereka cintai.”

**Key relationships:**
- Mira: Partner of trust. She grounds his questions with maps; he gives her permission to stop pretending everything can be charted.
- Kael: Respect that becomes mutual protection. Orin refuses to let Kael carry the border failure alone.
- Tavi: Curiosity. Orin is the first adult who doesn’t dismiss her “failed” toys.
- Evolution beats: Ch.5 (surge), Ch.14 (parents), Ch.20 (rejects Vaelor), Ch.28–30 (steward).

### Mira Solenne (`mira` / `character_mira_solenne`)
**Voice sample:**  
“Peta ini salah. Bukan karena aku menggambarnya buruk—karena tempat itu sudah tidak mau diingat.”

**Key relationships:**
- Orin: Equal partner. She teases his earnestness; he never laughs at her fear of imperfect maps.
- Kael: Formal at first, then quiet respect after he covers her during an archive raid.
- Iven: Teasing rivalry over routes. She draws; he walks. They end up needing both.
- Evolution: Maps as control → maps as living memory (Ch.14 sister reveal).

### Kael Rowan (`kael` / `character_kael_rowan`)
**Voice sample:**  
“Aku sudah menulis laporan itu tiga kali. Yang ketiga tidak pernah terkirim karena aku takut mereka benar—bahwa tidak ada yang perlu diingat.”

**Key relationships:**
- Orin & Mira: Protective. He positions himself between them and Crown agents without being asked.
- Tavi: Patience trial. Her inventions remind him of the “forgotten” tech he was ordered to ignore.
- Evolution: Guilt paralysis → chooses to trust the party over the Crown (Ch.18).

### Iven Marr (`iven` / `character_iven_marr`)
**Voice sample:**  
“Aku antar surat ke alamat yang kemarin masih ada. Hari ini pemiliknya bilang dia tidak pernah tinggal di situ.”

**Key relationships:**
- Mira: Route rivalry that becomes collaboration.
- Kael: Professional respect. Both have been told their memories are inconvenient.
- Evolution: Cynical courier → active witness (Ch.7–10).

### Tavi Brindle (`tavi` / `character_tavi_brindle`)
**Voice sample:**  
“Ini bukan mainan gagal. Ini cuma belum ingat caranya bernyanyi.”

**Key relationships:**
- Orin: Excitement. He listens.
- Sera (when present): Ethics clash over whether old tech should be touched.
- Evolution: Play → responsibility (Ch.8 toy echoes lattice).
