# Monster Lore Architecture (Phase E — Updated)
**Narrative law only. No stat or schema changes.**

## Categories
1. Common wildlife
2. Magical wildlife (Resonance-nesting)
3. Hostile creatures
4. Ancient guardians (lattice remnants)
5. Corrupted / Stillness-agitated
6. Rare / elite
7. Dungeon guardians
8. Major bosses (story-tied)

## Ecology Rules
- Habitat matches region climate and Resonance density.
- Stillness displaces nests; aggression is often disorientation, not malice.
- Cultures may revere or fear the same species differently.
- Later arcs introduce Stillness-touched and lattice-construct families rather than simple stat inflation.
- Bosses require narrative context (seal, experiment, forgotten oath, or Curator method).

## Arc Escalation Theme
- Arc I: Local wildlife + first guardians.
- Arc II: Political constructs and forgotten oaths.
- Arc III: Ancient verdant and underdeep remnants.
- Arc IV: Stillness agents and unwritten regents.
- Arc V: Echo and lattice scavengers.
- Arc VI: Husk, shardbearer, and final resonant forms.
