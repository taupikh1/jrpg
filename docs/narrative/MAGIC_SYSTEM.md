# Magic System — Resonance

## Definition
**Resonance** is the interaction between living beings, places, memories, and natural magical currents. It is not pure fuel.

## Rules
1. Resonance strengthens where memory, ritual, and place align.
2. Forced extraction destabilizes local memory (early Stillness symptoms).
3. Creatures may nest in high-Resonance nodes without being “evil.”
4. Technology of the ancients shaped Resonance into a **lattice**; damage to the lattice causes regional amnesia.
5. Combat “magic” is directed Resonance — limited by stamina/MP and emotional clarity.
6. Orin’s unusual link: he can feel lattice fractures and briefly stabilize or agitate them.

## Limitations
- Cannot arbitrarily rewrite large-scale history in a moment.
- Healing memory is not free (see chapter_17 costs).
- Antagonist’s Stillness is anti-Resonance: smoothing peaks by erasing anchors.

## Unstable Resonance
Symptoms: wrong maps, blank inscriptions, creatures circling empty nests, people mid-sentence losing names.
