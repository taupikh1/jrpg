# Dungeon Bible (Phase D)

## Arsip Bellhaven (bellhaven_archive_*)
- Reason: Bukti penghapusan sengaja.
- Flow: Hall → Stacks → Restricted.
- Optional: Archive Veilkeeper.
- Revelation: Sera trail; Accord policy seed.
- Return: Always open back to plaza.

## Situs Resonansi Kuno (resonance_ancient_site)
- Reason: Jejak fragmen Chapter 5.
- Flow: Pendekatan → pilar/instrumen/mural → inti.
- Boss: Warden of First Resonance (main).
- Revelation: Ancaman lebih besar; surge Orin.
- Soft-lock prevention: Exit to road_bellhaven always available after victory.

## Underdeep Holds (planned, region_underdeep)
- Reason: Teknologi lattice lama; masa lalu Bram.
- Conceptual areas: Segel tembaga, ruang mesin, arsip bawah.
- Mini-boss / hazards: Construct tua, gas resonansi.
- Revelation: Lattice sebagai penstabil memori.

## Perpustakaan Raja Tidur (planned)
- Reason: Sejarah kontradiktif.
- Puzzle concept: Rak yang isinya berubah sesuai siapa yang membaca.
- Revelation: Accord menulis ulang.

## Jalan Gema / Wayshrines (region_echo_road, planned)
- Reason: Resolusi misteri pribadi Arc V.
- Flow: Kuil kecil berurutan; nama berubah.
- Revelation: Detail insiden orang tua Orin dan saudara Mira.

## Heart of Lumerra Core (planned)
- Reason: Klimaks.
- Flow: Pendekatan berlapis → ruang lattice → bentuk akhir Vaelor.
- Boss: Vaelor’s Resonant Form.
- Soft-lock: Save points before final choice.

All dungeons have narrative reason and return path.
