# Story Architecture — Complete Spine (Stages 3)
**Status:** Newly consolidated. Expands MAIN_STORY_OUTLINE.md without contradiction.  
**Date:** 2026-11-14

## Core Theme
Memori yang menyakitkan vs “kedamaian” yang menghapus. Stillness adalah solusi palsu untuk trauma kolektif. Party harus memilih apakah dunia layak mengingat luka-lukanya.

## Arc Structure
| Arc | Chapters | Central Question | Climax |
|-----|----------|------------------|--------|
| I — The Awakening | 1–5 | Apakah lupa ini alami? | Orin’s first Resonance surge |
| II — The Shattered Kingdoms | 6–10 | Siapa yang sengaja menghapus? | Night of Falling Stars / Oathbreaker |
| III — Echoes of the Ancients | 11–15 | Apa yang sebenarnya terjadi 800 tahun lalu? | First Silence partial truth |
| IV — The Rising Darkness | 16–20 | Siapa Curator dan apa yang dia inginkan? | Vaelor reveal (Ch.20) |
| V — The Road of Forgotten Echoes | 21–25 | Bisakah lattice diperbaiki tanpa menghapus? | Lattice purpose revealed |
| VI — The Last Stars | 26–30 | Apa harga dari mengingat? | Final confrontation + endings |

## Character Arc Spine (principal)
- **Orin:** Reactive helper → steward of hard truths. Personal: parents’ “quiet storm”.
- **Mira:** Control via maps → living memory. Personal: sister’s vanishing.
- **Kael:** Guilt-bound soldier → protector who trusts again. Personal: falsified border report.
- **Iven:** Cynical courier → witness who chooses sides. Personal: routes that disappear.
- **Tavi:** Inventor who plays → inventor who understands consequence. Personal: toy that echoes lattice.
- **Sera / Neris / Bram:** Knowledge keepers whose truths collide (written vs oral vs lived).

## Antagonist Arc
Vaelor Senn (Curator): Tragedy → ideology of surgical forgetting → confrontation. Not pure evil; his solution is rejected.

## Mystery Payoff Map
- Stillness symptoms (Ch.1) → full cosmology (Arc V–VI)
- Erased archives (Ch.4) → Accord policy (Ch.9+) → resolution Ch.23–28
- Mira’s sister → lattice fold (Ch.14–21)
- Kael’s report → falsified for crown (Ch.18)

- Lattice purpose → memory stabilizer (Ch.22–25)
- Orin’s parents → lattice incident (Ch.21–24)

2. **Quiet Mercy** — accept partial Stillness (specific flags).
