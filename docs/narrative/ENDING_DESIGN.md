# Ending Design
**Phase B.** Two to three endings per existing intent. All consistent with central theme.

## Ending A — Memory with Scars (Default)
- **Conditions:** Reject Vaelor’s offer in Ch.20; do not accept partial Stillness in late flags; complete main story.
- **Player decisions:** Consistently choose remembrance over erasure.
- **Character consequences:** Orin becomes steward of hard truths. Mira keeps living maps. Kael releases guilt. Others retain their scars and bonds.
- **World consequences:** Stillness recedes. Villages begin recovering forgotten names. Residual scars remain.
- **Final confrontation:** Vaelor defeated; his wound acknowledged but solution rejected.
- **Closing narration (Bahasa Indonesia):**  
  “Langit di atas Ashwillow kembali biru. Di balai desa, seorang tua mencoba menyanyikan himne pendiri. Namanya masih goyah di lidahnya—tapi kali ini ia tidak menyerah. Ingatan itu sakit. Dan karena itu, ia layak dipertahankan.”
- **Post-ending:** Letters and visits between party members. World rebuilds slowly.

## Ending B — Quiet Mercy
- **Conditions:** Accept at least one major Stillness concession (specific late-game flag, e.g. after Ch.28 choice).
- **Player decisions:** Agree that some memories are too dangerous to keep.
- **Character consequences:** Some personal wounds are softened or lost. Relationships feel quieter, slightly distant.
- **World consequences:** Conflict decreases. Certain names and places remain permanently blurred.
- **Final confrontation:** Vaelor is stopped from full erasure but partial Stillness takes hold.
- **Closing narration:**  
  “Desa-desa menjadi lebih tenang. Tidak ada yang bertengkar tentang masa lalu—karena sebagian dari masa lalu itu sudah tidak ada. Orin berdiri di sumur tua dan tidak bisa mengingat nama festival yang pernah ia janji untuk pulihkan.”
- **Post-ending:** More isolated epilogues. Some party members no longer fully recognize shared moments.

## Ending C — Unwritten Dawn (Hidden)
- **Conditions:** High relationship values with all recruited members + complete all personal quests + reject both full Stillness and simple destruction of the lattice.
- **Player decisions:** Pursue the third path discovered in Ch.25–29.
- **Character consequences:** Party finds a way to repair without erasing. Scars remain but no longer spread.
- **World consequences:** Lattice stabilized as a living memory network. Stillness ends. New, careful stewardship begins.
- **Final confrontation:** Vaelor confronted with the possibility he refused to see; tragic clarity possible.
- **Closing narration:**  
  “Bintang-bintang yang pernah jatuh kini diam di tempatnya. Bukan karena dilupakan—karena seseorang akhirnya bersedia menulisnya kembali, lengkap dengan lukanya.”
- **Post-ending:** Fullest epilogue. Recurring NPCs remember. Festival names return.

No ending invalidates earlier character development or contradicts the theme that memory, even painful, has value.
