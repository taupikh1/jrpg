# Antagonist Bible — The Curator / Vaelor Senn

**IDs:** `character_vaelor_senn`, `antagonist_curator`

## Identity
Vaelor Senn, once a Resonance scholar tied to post-Accord research circles. Public mask: **The Curator**, collector of “dangerous memories.”

## Tragedy
Lost family in a lattice failure that official history minimized. He remembers what kingdoms ordered forgotten.

## Ideology
Painful memory → cycles of vengeance. Stillness as surgical removal of those loops. Understandable; not justifiable.

## Relationship to Orin
Sees in Orin a mirror: sensitivity without “his” bitterness. Tries to recruit, then to overwrite.

## Resolution
Defeated without pure demonization; epilogue acknowledges his wound while rejecting his solution.
