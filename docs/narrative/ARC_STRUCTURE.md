## Pacing Beats
- Opening hook: Ch.1 elder forgets founder name.
- Early escalation: Ch.2–4 carvings and erased archive.
- First emotional turn: Ch.5 Resonance surge + Ch.14 personal wounds.
- Mid-game: Ch.10 organization revealed; Ch.15 ancient catastrophe confirmed.
- Antagonist reveal: Chapter 20 (Vaelor).
- Late truth: Ch.22–25 lattice purpose and personal resolutions.
- Final confrontation: Ch.28–30.
- Epilogue: dependent on ending.

No existing arc names replaced.
