# World Master Plan (Phase D)
**Preserves all 12 existing region IDs and playable zone IDs.**

## Global Geography
Lumerra consists of soft river valleys in the south (Ashwillow), temperate forests, trade hills (Bellhaven), cool crown plains, border moors, a coastal invention city, open starfall grasslands, lush ruin-jungles, subterranean holds, highland mists, scarred echo roads, and the anomalous Heart.

## Travel & Progression
- Arc I: Ashwillow → Whispering Wood → Bellhaven road → Archive → Resonance site (all playable).
- Arc II: Crownspire → Silent March → Glass Lantern → Starfall Plain (planned zones).
- Arc III: Verdant Veil → Underdeep → Sleeping Kings.
- Arc V: Echo Road.
- Arc VI: Heart of Lumerra.

Story-gated routes open after chapter flags (e.g. northern path after Ch.2, crown road after Ch.5).

## No New Top-Level Regions
All story needs are met inside the existing 12. Additional landmarks are sub-locations only.
