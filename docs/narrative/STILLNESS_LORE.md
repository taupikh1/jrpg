# The Stillness

## Origin (Canon Truth — reveal paced)
Centuries ago, ancient builders over-tuned the Resonance lattice to preserve a golden age of memory. A catastrophe (the First Silence) fractured the lattice. An **Accord** between kingdoms buried the technology and the truth. Vaelor Senn later concludes that pain-linked memories drive cycles of war; he seeks to complete a controlled Stillness to “spare” the world.

## Symptoms (early → late)
1. Local name loss, festival gaps
2. Record erasure / map drift
3. Creature disorientation
4. Border treaty amnesia
5. Regional catastrophe (stars dimming metaphorically and literally in lattice light)
6. Edge of Stillness — mass identity risk

## Antagonist Interpretation
Stillness = mercy. Remove anchors of grief so people cannot reenact harm.

## Actual Cost
Without anchors, culture, love, and choice collapse. Stopping Stillness requires accepting imperfect memory and repairing lattice nodes — not total reversal of every loss.

## How It Stops
Party restores Heart of Lumerra balance (chapter_25–30); Vaelor’s apparatus is dismantled; residual scars remain (epilogue honesty).
