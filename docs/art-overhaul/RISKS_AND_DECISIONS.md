# Risks and Owner Decisions (Milestone 0)

## Risks
1. **Licensing** — Majority of useful assets are LICENSE_UNVERIFIED. MapleStory-named content is high-risk.
2. **Style consistency** — Packs come from different sources; may look mixed without curation.
3. **Missing heroes** — No obvious ready party sheets; may need careful selection or limited procedural hybrid.
4. **Scope** — Full overhaul of every zone/enemy/UI is large; must stay milestone-gated.
5. **Mobile performance** — Large atlases or the 31k icon set could hurt iPhone 13.

## Decisions Needed Before Milestone 1
1. Approve proceeding with only assets that pass a deeper license check (exclude MapleStory by default)?
2. Accept hybrid approach (new tiles/enemies where verified + procedural fallback for missing party sheets)?
3. Choose priority zone for prototype (recommend village or archive hall).
4. Confirm no engine change and no BGM.

## Current Recommendation
Yes to 1, 2, and 4. Prototype on one Arc I zone. Do not touch story, save, or runtime IDs.
